English

As of this policy’s effective date, the current version of ShiftVista is an offline application. Your schedules, wage settings, notes, adjustments and custom templates are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer or third parties. The current version does not provide accounts, cloud sync or advertising, does not connect to a developer-operated server, and does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: used only for start, end and next-day shift reminders that you configure.
  • Start after reboot: used to rebuild local reminders after the device restarts.
  • Clipboard: accessed only when you choose to copy a backup code or paste one for restoration. Clipboard contents are not sent to the developer.
  • Backup files: a system file picker is used only when you choose to save or restore a backup file. ShiftVista can access only the file location you select.
  • Google Play rating: opens the Google Play app or website. ShiftVista itself does not request the Internet permission.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Backup codes and files are controlled by you; anyone who receives a valid backup may be able to read the schedule stored in it.

Children

The application is intended for working-age users and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds advertising, subscriptions, accounts, cloud sync, analytics or crash reporting, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released. The update will identify the data involved, its purpose, relevant third-party services and available user choices. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by another appropriate method. Questions: liu.dev2026@gmail.com.
