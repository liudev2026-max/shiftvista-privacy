English

ShiftVista is an ad-supported schedule app. Schedules, wages, dates, shift times, notes, custom templates, and backup codes or files stay on your device and are not uploaded. There is currently no cloud schedule backup, synchronization, or subscription.

Google Mobile Ads and UMP request consent and serve ads. Depending on choices, region, and settings, Google may collect and share approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers including the Advertising ID for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention or security. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are optional and off by default. Enabled Analytics may collect lifecycle, session, engagement and interaction data, technical app/device information, and app-instance or device identifiers. Crashlytics may process crash logs, diagnostics, performance information, and identifiers; reports are not uploaded by default, and disabling it requests deletion of pending unsent reports. Google processes SDK data under its terms.

Optional Google sign-in lets Google and Firebase Authentication process your name, email address, and Firebase Authentication user ID to create, maintain, and delete the account. ShiftVista does not send these account fields to Analytics or Crashlytics. Privacy choices and separate Analytics/Crashlytics switches are in Settings; Analytics additionally requires UMP Consent Mode permission. Crashlytics opt-out fully applies next start. TLS protects network data.

Local data remains until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Account data remains until in-app deletion or a verified request through the deletion page. Google retains advertising, consent, analytics, and diagnostics under its service terms and controls; account deletion does not promise deletion of data separately controlled by Google. Contact liu.dev2026@gmail.com.
