Delete your ShiftVista account

Privacy policy

English

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and sign in again with the same Google account. This deletes the Firebase Authentication account, not your Google Account or schedules on your device. Clear app storage or uninstall ShiftVista to remove local data. If the app is unavailable, email liu.dev2026@gmail.com from the same Google account. After verification, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account within 30 days. ShiftVista currently has no cloud schedule backup.
